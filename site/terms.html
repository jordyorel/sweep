Using Sweep

  • Sweep moves files to the Trash and lets you undo. Files are only gone for good once you empty the Trash.
  • Review what Sweep selects before removing it, especially items marked as shared or system-owned.
  • Keep regular backups (for example with Time Machine). No uninstaller can guarantee that every removal is right for your setup.

Malware checks

Sweep's security features are on-demand checks based on Apple's XProtect signatures and code-signing information. They are not real-time antivirus protection and cannot detect every threat. A clean result does not guarantee that your Mac is free of malware.
