Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Sweep is a free, open-source macOS app made by Jordy Orel Konda. This page explains what data Sweep handles. Short version: Sweep collects nothing and sends nothing.

The app

  • No data collection. Sweep has no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no ads.
  • No network requests. Sweep's own code never connects to the internet. Uninstalls, launch-item audits and malware scans run entirely on your Mac.
  • Apple's own checks. To show whether an app is signed and notarized, Sweep asks macOS's built-in tools (codesign and Gatekeeper's spctl). As when you open any app, macOS may contact Apple to verify a notarization. Sweep does not add anything to those checks.
  • Malware signatures. Scans use Apple's XProtect rules already installed on your Mac. Sweep reads them locally and does not download or upload anything.
  • Files Sweep reads. To find leftovers and check for malware, Sweep reads file names, metadata and, during scans, file contents in the locations you choose. This information stays in memory or on your Mac and is never transmitted.
  • Files Sweep stores. Sweep keeps a small undo history and, if you turn on Background Watch, a list of known launch items and alerts. These live in ~/Library/Application Support/sweep. Deleting that folder removes them. Background Watch also installs a small launch agent, ~/Library/LaunchAgents/dev.sweep.watch.plist, removed when you turn the watch off.
  • Administrator password. When you remove system files, macOS asks for your password in its own dialog. Sweep never sees or stores it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit or download a release; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Donations

Donations are made in cryptocurrency directly to the addresses on the home page. Sweep receives no personal data from you, but blockchain transactions are public by nature.

Changes
